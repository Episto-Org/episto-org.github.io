    Invite someone
    
  
    1. Make an invitation
    
  
    A one-time invitation, signed with your key so it can only be yours. Send the link (or the code) to the person you invite, privately. It works once, for 30 days. Making one costs nothing: an invitation counts against your limit (2 a year for members; more for chairs and librarians, see the rules) only when the person is added.

    Lost your password?
    
  
    Nobody can read your password, Episto included, but you can replace it. Your pseudonym stays the same.
    
  
    With your recovery code
    
  
    The code you wrote down when you joined, or when you last changed your password. This makes a sealed request; put it in a pull request of its own, opened from your own GitHub account, the one registered to you. The steward tells that account in the Key changes issue and switches to your new password 7 days later. A change you did not ask for is stopped by commenting /cancel there.

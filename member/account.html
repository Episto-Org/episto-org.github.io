    Lost your password?
    
  
    Nobody can recover it, Episto included. Keep your pseudonym and choose a new password: this makes your new key, here in your browser. Your entry then needs the new key line, in place of the old one.
    
  
    Founders change the line themselves. Other members change it in a pull request that touches only that line; the maintainers approve it once your inviter has confirmed with you, in person or the private way your invitation came, that the change is yours.

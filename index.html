Check a reference list

Paste the references of a paper, a manuscript or a reading list. Each one is checked against retractions, official notices and flags verified by the Episto community.

Any citation style, BibTeX, RIS, or a list of DOIs.
(optional, shows citations made after a flag)

🔒 Checked in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

    How to read the results

    CriticalThe flagged result cannot be relied on at all, for example a retraction.
    MajorThe flagged conclusion is not supported as stated. Parts of the work may still hold.
    CautionThe result may hold, but should not be relied on without the stated limitation.
    Builds on flagged workNot flagged itself, but cites work flagged Critical or Major.

    Match confidence. DOI match is certain. Title match is very likely, but verify it. Possible match means most words of the title match; check it by hand. References without a DOI match less reliably, so add DOIs where you can.

    A flag describes a specific problem with specific work. It is not a judgement of the authors, and citing flagged work can be legitimate, for example to discuss it.

    Episto flags specific problems with specific research, decided by randomly drawn panels of pseudonymous reviewers. Flags are not judgements of people.
